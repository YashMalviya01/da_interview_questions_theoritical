# Part B — Theory: 15 Questions with Solutions

## Q16. What is data cleaning, and why is it important?

**Solution / interview answer:** Data cleaning identifies and handles inaccurate, incomplete, inconsistent, duplicate, or incorrectly formatted data. It improves the reliability of analysis.

## Q17. What is the difference between a population and a sample?

**Solution / interview answer:** A population is the complete group being studied; a sample is a subset selected from that population for analysis or estimation.

## Q18. Explain different types of sampling methods.

**Solution / interview answer:** Common methods are simple random, systematic, stratified, and cluster sampling. The appropriate method depends on population structure and the analytical objective.

## Q19. What is the difference between mean, median, and mode?

**Solution / interview answer:** Mean is the arithmetic average, median is the middle sorted value, and mode is the most frequent value. Median is generally less affected by extreme values.

## Q20. What is Standard Deviation?

**Solution / interview answer:** Standard deviation measures how dispersed observations are around the mean. A larger value indicates greater variability.

## Q21. Why is Standard Deviation important?

**Solution / interview answer:** It adds information about variability that an average alone cannot provide, helping compare consistency, volatility, or risk.

## Q22. What is Regression Analysis?

**Solution / interview answer:** Regression examines relationships between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables and can be used for explanation, estimation, or prediction.

## Q23. When would you use Regression Analysis?

**Solution / interview answer:** Use regression when you want to quantify relationships between variables or predict a numerical outcome, while checking whether model assumptions are appropriate.

## Q24. What is Simple Random Sampling?

**Solution / interview answer:** Every member of the population has an equal probability of selection. It is straightforward when a suitable sampling frame exists.

## Q25. What is Systematic Sampling?

**Solution / interview answer:** Select observations at a fixed interval after a starting point, such as every 10th record. Periodic patterns should be considered.

## Q26. What is Stratified Sampling?

**Solution / interview answer:** Divide the population into meaningful subgroups and sample from each subgroup so important groups are represented.

## Q27. What is Cluster Sampling?

**Solution / interview answer:** Divide the population into natural groups and select some groups for study. It can reduce collection cost when populations are geographically clustered.

## Q28. How would you handle missing values statistically?

**Solution / interview answer:** First investigate the missingness pattern. Depending on context, use mean/median/mode imputation, model-based or multiple imputation, or remove observations when justified.

## Q29. How would you detect outliers?

**Solution / interview answer:** Use IQR, Z-scores, box plots, distributions, and business rules. Investigate whether an outlier is a genuine observation before removing it.

## Q30. What is the IQR method for detecting outliers?

**Solution / interview answer:** IQR = Q3 − Q1. Potential outliers are below Q1 − 1.5×IQR or above Q3 + 1.5×IQR.
